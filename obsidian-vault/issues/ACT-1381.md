# ACT-1381: Zalrsc- SC result after same-hart store is over-constrained in cp_custom_sc_after_store

cp_custom_sc_after_store required SC success after a same-hart store into the reservation set; either result is legal.

## What fails

Zalrsc-sc.w/sc.d SC returns 1 on hardware after a same-hart store.

## What the maintainers concluded

The spec says nothing about same-hart stores; ACT dropped the bin (#1476) and Sail added a config option (SAIL-1678).

## How triage treats a match

Not a hardware bug; the test no longer exists in current ACT.
