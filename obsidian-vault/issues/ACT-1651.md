# ACT-1651: xenvcfg should only be accessed with priv 1.12 and newer

Test generation accessed menvcfg/senvcfg on every core; they exist only from privileged 1.12, so priv-1.11 cores trap.

## What fails

An illegal-instruction trap on a menvcfg/senvcfg/henvcfg access in S/Ssstrict/Interrupts tests on a priv-1.11 board.

## What the maintainers concluded

Maintainers: generation follows the ratified spec but may exclude by priv version; xenvcfg accesses are gated like mstatush (#ifndef SM1P11P0_SUPPORTED). Sail gates them too (SAIL-1753).

## How triage treats a match

On a priv-1.11 board (VF2 U74, Megrez P550) an xenvcfg access that traps is expected: known deviation, not a hardware bug.
