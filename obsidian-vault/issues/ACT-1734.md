# ACT-1734: ExceptionsF: RVTEST_SIGUPD_CSR_READ causes a second trap after CSRRS/CSRRC on FS=Off implementations

With mstatus.FS=Off, RVTEST_SIGUPD_CSR_READ issued a second CSR read after CSRRS/CSRRC, causing a second trap.

## What fails

ExceptionsF-00 records an extra illegal-instruction trap after an FP CSR coverpoint.

## What the maintainers concluded

The macro re-read the CSR unnecessarily; fixed in the framework.

## How triage treats a match

If seen again, the ACT revision predates the fix.
