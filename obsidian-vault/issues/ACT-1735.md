# ACT-1735: cp_instr_adr_misaligned_jalr generator produces incorrect JALR landing addresses for rs1[1:0]=01, offset[1:0]=01 coverage bin

cp_instr_adr_misaligned_jalr generated a JALR that skipped the padding instruction for rs1[1:0]=01, offset[1:0]=01.

## What fails

The JALR lands on the upper half of the next auipc (c.illegal) and raises an unexpected illegal instruction.

## What the maintainers concluded

Generator bug, fixed upstream.

## How triage treats a match

If seen again, the ACT revision predates the fix.
