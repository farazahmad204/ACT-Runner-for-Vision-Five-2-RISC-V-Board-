# ACT-1782: Make medeleg configurable in rvtest_boot_to_smode for DUT-specific WARL implementations

rvtest_boot_to_smode wrote a fixed medeleg value assuming all causes are delegatable.

## What fails

Tests that boot to S-mode diverge when the board cannot delegate some causes.

## What the maintainers concluded

Deferred to the Sail delegation masks (SAIL-1781, sail 0.13). Open question in ACT: how the M-mode abstraction handles DUTs that cannot delegate everything.

## How triage treats a match

Make sail.json's delegation mask match the measured medeleg mask.
