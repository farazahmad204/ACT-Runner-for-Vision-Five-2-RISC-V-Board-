# ACT-1828: [ ACT/UDB Profile ] ] Sstvecd-00 false failure on VF2/U74 due to unmodeled stvec.BASE WARL canonicalization

Sstvecd-00 writes illegal (noncanonical) addresses to stvec; the U74 legalizes the BASE high bits (WARL), so the jump goes elsewhere and the trap signature differs.

## What fails

Sstvecd-00 fails on VF2 with mcause=1 and mtval=0xffffffc000000000 (instruction access fault at the canonicalized address).

## What the maintainers concluded

Maintainers: known test limitation. They plan to add a Sail parameter for the legalization or stop testing illegal addresses. Same conclusion for sepc/mepc (SAIL-1843).

## How triage treats a match

Known ACT limitation on VF2: verdict 'Test or ACT issue', not a hardware bug.
