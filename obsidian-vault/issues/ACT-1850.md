# ACT-1850: ACT Sv39 canonical tests reject legal implementation-defined stval conversion on SiFive U74

sv39_canonical_Smode/Umode require stval to hold the exact noncanonical address; the U74 may write another invalid address.

## What fails

stval after the page fault on a noncanonical address differs from the Sail value on VF2.

## What the maintainers concluded

The spec lets xtval hold a converted 'invalid' address (XLEN-1..VA bits not all equal) instead of the full value. Open in ACT.

## How triage treats a match

A different-but-invalid stval on VF2 is legal: verdict 'Test or ACT issue'.
