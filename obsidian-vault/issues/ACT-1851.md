# ACT-1851: ACT S-mode failure handler reads M-mode CSRs and enters an infinite illegal-instruction trap loop

On an S-mode trap-signature mismatch the failure routine read M-mode CSRs from S-mode and looped on illegal-instruction traps.

## What fails

A test that fails in S-mode never reports and times out (endless illegal-instruction traps in failedtest_trap_x7_x9).

## What the maintainers concluded

Fixed by #1955.

## How triage treats a match

A timeout after an S-mode mismatch with this loop means the ACT revision predates #1955.
