# ACT-1880: PMPS/PMPU tests hardcode PMP entry 15 and abort on targets with 8 usable entries

PMPS/PMPU tests use PMP entry 15 as a background region; boards with 8 PMP entries ignore the writes.

## What fails

pmps_*/pmpu_* tests abort after entering S-mode because no PMP entry grants access.

## What the maintainers concluded

Duplicate of #1721: tests assume 16 PMP entries.

## How triage treats a match

On a board with 8 usable PMP entries (VF2) these failures are a test limitation.
