# ACT-1898: pmpzalrsc_cfg_wr expects success from an unconstrained LR/SC sequence

pmpzalrsc_cfg_wr puts RVTEST_SIGUPD between LR and SC, so the sequence is unconstrained and SC may fail legally.

## What fails

SC returns nonzero on VF2 although PMP allows the access.

## What the maintainers concluded

ACT now uses constrained sequences where success is expected; this test was missed and is being updated.

## How triage treats a match

SC failure in this test is legal: verdict 'Test or ACT issue'.
