# ACT-1900: SsstrictSm-04 models mconfigptr as present on a Privileged ISA 1.11 U74 target

SsstrictSm-04 sweeps mconfigptr (0xF15), which priv-1.11 cores do not have.

## What fails

One extra illegal-instruction trap on the board compared with Sail.

## What the maintainers concluded

Sail 0.13 gates mconfigptr on the privileged version; configure the board as priv 1.11.

## How triage treats a match

Expected on priv 1.11; fix the Sail config if it still differs.
