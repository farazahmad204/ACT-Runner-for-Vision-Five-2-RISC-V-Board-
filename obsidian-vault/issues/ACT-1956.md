# ACT-1956: InterruptsS-00 :: U-mode WFI subtest races MTIP delivery on hardware

The U-mode WFI subtest does not wait for MTIP before cleanup; on VF2 the mtimecmp/MTIP propagation is slower than in Sail.

## What fails

InterruptsS-00 (and InterruptsSm-00 on MTIP deassertion) fails intermittently on VF2 in the timer/WFI subtest.

## What the maintainers concluded

Open: the test races asynchronous timer delivery; needs a wait for mip.MTIP.

## How triage treats a match

Treat as a test timing issue on VF2 unless it fails consistently.
