# ACT-2010: Sm_mcause-00 assumes exact readback for unsupported WLRL mcause cause 19

Sm_mcause-00 writes reserved exception cause 19 and requires exact readback; the U74 reads 0x3.

## What fails

Sm_mcause-00 bin b_19: x9 = 0x3, expected 0x13.

## What the maintainers concluded

Reserved/WLRL values need not read back. #2134 stops testing the hardware-error cause without Sm 1.13.

## How triage treats a match

Legal on priv 1.11: known deviation (or old ACT revision).
