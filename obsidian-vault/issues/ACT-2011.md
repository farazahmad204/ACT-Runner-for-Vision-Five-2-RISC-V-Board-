# ACT-2011: Sm_mcsr_cntr-00 assumes 64-bit HPM counters; add a per-counter width parameter

Sm_mcsr_cntr-00 assumes full-XLEN HPM counters; the U74's are 40 bits.

## What fails

mhpmcounter3/4 read back the written value truncated to 40 bits on VF2.

## What the maintainers concluded

Agreed: needs a per-counter width parameter; UDB support was added, Sail/ACT follow-up open.

## How triage treats a match

40-bit counter readback on VF2 is legal: verdict 'Test or ACT issue'.
