# ACT-2206: [Bug] ExceptionsZalrsc assumes bus fault on an sc.w that may not issue to the bus.

ExceptionsZalrsc issues lr.w + sc.w to RVMODEL_ACCESS_FAULT_ADDRESS and asserts the sc.w traps; on a core where the lr.w traps and the unpaired sc.w never reaches the bus, sc.w just fails without a fault.

## What fails

ExceptionsZalrsc-00: the board takes the lr.w access fault but no sc.w fault, Sail takes both.

## What the maintainers concluded

Open. Proposed: use a read-only address so lr.w succeeds and sc.w really issues. Blocked on the architectural question in #3350.

## How triage treats a match

A missing sc.w access fault after a faulting lr.w is legal behavior: verdict 'Test or ACT issue'.
