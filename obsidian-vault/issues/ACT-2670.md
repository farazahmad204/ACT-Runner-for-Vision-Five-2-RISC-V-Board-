# ACT-2670: xtinst check is blocked by sail-riscv#1982

xtinst checks are relaxed: TRAP_SIGUPD_ZERO_OK accepts a zero xtinst even where the H spec forbids it (guest-page fault from an implicit VS-stage access with nonzero htval/mtval2), until sail-riscv#1982 lets Sail write transformed instructions.

## What fails

xtinst tests compare less than the spec requires; trap signature word 5 is not compared on explicit-access traps.

## What the maintainers concluded

Open, blocked on sail-riscv#1982. UDB's TINST_VALUE_ON_* parameters will make the compare exact.

## How triage treats a match

A pass does not prove xtinst is right; a failure in another signature word (e.g. word 0, Vector+Mode+Status) is a different problem.
