# ACT-2717: [Bug] Hypervisor tests on real hardware: Milk-V Megrez (SiFive P550, H draft 0.6, priv 1.11): 123 built, 84 ran, 54 pass

Our report of the H tests on the Megrez. The maintainer traced the 39 SvH/SvHSm self-check failures to the board config: the UDB yaml declares S/Sm 1.12 (needed to generate H tests), so SvH writes menvcfg.ADUE/PBMTE through T-SBI; the P550 (and Sail with Privileged_ISA_1_11) has no menvcfg, the write traps inside T-SBI dispatch and the test loops until the trap signature overflows.

## What fails

SvH_*/SvHSm_* (sv39x4/sv48x4 and VS-stage translation) fail with 'Trap signature overflow ... Increase TRAP_SIGUPD_COUNT' on Sail, and would trap on menvcfg on the board.

## What the maintainers concluded

davidharrishmc (2026-10-06): all 39 pass on sail-rv64-max and with Privileged_ISA_1_12; raising TRAP_SIGUPD_COUNT would not help. These tests cannot run on a priv-1.11 hart as written. ACT may make a trapping T-SBI instruction fail with a clear message instead of looping. Use `act -k` to see all failures.

## How triage treats a match

Known deviation on the Megrez (priv 1.11, no menvcfg): not a hardware bug. Other H-test failures on the board still need their own analysis.
