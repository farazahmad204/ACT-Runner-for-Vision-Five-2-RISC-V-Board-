# SAIL-1753: Add privileged-spec version gated control for xenvcfg CSR availability in Sail

Sail exposed menvcfg/senvcfg/henvcfg regardless of the configured privileged version; priv 1.11 cores trap on them.

## What fails

A CSR access to menvcfg/senvcfg/henvcfg traps (illegal instruction) on a priv-1.11 board while Sail reads it.

## What the maintainers concluded

Sail gates xenvcfg on privileged_isa_version since #1756. Tests must also gate the accesses on S/Sm 1.12 (ACT-1651).

## How triage treats a match

On a priv-1.11 board an xenvcfg access that traps is expected. If Sail reads it, the board's sail.json privileged_isa_version is wrong.
