# SAIL-1781: Add configurable medeleg (and mideleg) WARL masks in sail.json

Sail assumed every medeleg/mideleg bit is writable; the U74 implements fewer delegatable bits, so Sail delegated traps the hardware handles in M-mode.

## What fails

Trap-signature mismatches where Sail shows a trap delegated to S-mode and the board handles it in M-mode.

## What the maintainers concluded

medeleg/mideleg are WARL. Sail 0.13 added configurable delegation masks in sail.json (PR #1790); ACT-1782 deferred to this.

## How triage treats a match

Check the board's sail.json delegatable-bits mask against the measured medeleg mask before suspecting hardware.
