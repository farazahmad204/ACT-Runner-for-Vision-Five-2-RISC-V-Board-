# SAIL-1843: Add configurable xepc WARL legalization for target-specific invalid addresses

Writing a noncanonical Sv39 address (0x0000008000000000) to sepc reads back canonicalized (0xffffff8000000000) on the U74; Sail keeps the written value.

## What fails

S_scsr-00 compares sepc/mepc readback of a noncanonical address against Sail's unmodified value.

## What the maintainers concluded

Agreed in the ACT meeting: xepc/stvec readback of illegal addresses is a test limitation (WARL legalization is implementation-defined), not a Sail bug. Tracked in ACT-1828.

## How triage treats a match

Treat a canonicalized xepc readback of a noncanonical address as legal WARL behavior.
